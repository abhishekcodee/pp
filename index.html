The Gap We Close

Two Things Can Be True at the Same Time.

Manufacturers can't find people who are ready to work. Technically qualified graduates can't find opportunities that arrive. PRAYAS is the bridge between the two.

🎓 Education Teaches Knowledge

Classrooms explain concepts and theory — a vital first step, but not the same as shop-floor performance.

🏭 Industry Demands Performance

Factories need people who can wire, diagnose and commission equipment safely, on day one, under real pressure.

Why PRAYAS Exists

The Gap Isn't About Talent. It's About Exposure.

A student may understand electrical theory, yet hesitate while wiring a live control panel. A diploma graduate may recognise a PLC diagram, yet feel uncertain troubleshooting a real machine. These moments don't reveal a lack of ability — they reveal a lack of opportunity to practise before responsibility arrives.

  • Confidence is earned one practical challenge at a time
  • Mentors close the gap classrooms leave open
  • Every learner practises on live, industrial-grade equipment
The Manufacturing Opportunity

India Is Entering Its Most Significant Manufacturing Transformation Yet.

New factories are being built. Existing ones are modernising. Automation is becoming more accessible and digital manufacturing is accelerating — but none of it moves forward without people who can operate, maintain and improve it.

  • MSMEs are scaling faster than they can staff
  • Automation is raising the skill bar, not lowering demand for people
  • This is bigger than employment — it's India's manufacturing future

Before You Apply or Partner

How do I know which pathway is right for me? +
A PRAYAS learning advisor reviews your background — ITI, diploma or engineering — and your career goals, then recommends the right fit.
Do I need prior industrial experience? +
No. Most students join straight from ITI or diploma college — that's exactly who this is built for.
Do manufacturing companies actually hire from PRAYAS? +
Yes — PRAYAS graduates are hired directly through our placement pipeline across partner manufacturers.
Is there a cost to partnering with PRAYAS as an MSME? +
We offer both hiring-partner models for talent acquisition and customised fee-based workforce upskilling programmes tailored to specific factory requirements.
